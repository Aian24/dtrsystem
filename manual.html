System Architecture

Automated biometric attendance processing pipeline.

General

The DTR Management System is an automated attendance platform designed to parse raw biometric scanner punch records, calculate work hours, lates, breaks, and undertimes, and produce official printable Daily Time Records (DTR) and consolidated reports.

Biometric Log Upload & Direct Device Sync

Sync attendance directly from your IntelliSmart fingerprint machine or upload decrypted logs.

Admin Side

The system provides two seamless ways to get attendance logs into the database: Direct Network Sync over IP (no extra software needed) and Universal Log Decryption for USB exports.

fingerprint Method 1: Direct Network Biometric Sync (Recommended)

Connect directly to your IntelliSmart or ZKTeco machine across your local network:

1
Connect Biometric Machine to Network / PC
Connect your biometric device to your local network switch/router using an Ethernet (LAN) cable (or directly into your PC's LAN port). Ensure your computer and the biometric machine are on the same subnet (e.g. 192.168.1.xxx).
2
Click "Sync from Device (IP)"
On the Upload Logs page, click the blue Sync from Device (IP) button in the top banner.
3
Test Connection & Sync
Confirm your device's IP Address (e.g. 192.168.1.201) and Port (4370). Click Test Connection to verify communication, then click Sync Now. The system will download all attendance logs, decode them, and insert them into the database automatically.

lock_open Method 2: USB Export & Universal Log Decryption

If you download logs via a USB flash drive, the built-in universal decryptor automatically unpacks binary structures, encrypted streams, and Chinese character encodings:

// Supported File Types (Auto-Decrypted):
• .dat / attlog.dat — Binary packed C-structures from ZKTeco/IntelliSmart USB exports
• Chinese Encodings — GBK, GB2312, GB18030, Big5, UTF-16 (automatically fixes Mojibake)
• XOR Scrambled Files — Automatically unmasked and converted
• .log / .txt / .csv — Standard delimited punch records
1
Drag & Drop Any Log File
Drag your attlog.dat, .log, .txt, or .csv file into the upload zone or click Select Files.
2
Click "Process & Decrypt Logs"
Click the Process & Decrypt Logs button. The universal decryptor automatically detects the format, decrypts it, and inserts all records.
3
Review Summary
A summary dialog displays the count of imported records, duplicate records, and invalid lines.

functions

Biometric Calculation Rules

Algorithmic rules for punch pairing, grace periods, lates, and rest days.

Core Engine

1. Punch Pairing

First 'I' punch = Time In.
Last 'O' punch = Time Out.
Intermediate punches are assigned to 1st Break Out/In and 2nd Break Out/In.

2. Late Calculation

Late triggers if Time In > (Work Start + Grace Period).
Late Minutes = Time In - Work Start.
Example: 8:00 AM start + 10m grace; punch at 8:15 AM = 15 mins late.

3. Rest Day vs Absence

Days with zero punches are marked Rest Day if the day matches the employee's rest schedule. Otherwise, marked Absent.

Punch Pairing State Machine

Recorded Punches on Day Matched Field Assignment Calculated Status
2 Punches (e.g. 07:55 I, 17:00 O) Time In = 07:55 AM • Time Out = 05:00 PM Present (Normal Shift)
4 Punches (07:50 I, 12:00 O, 13:00 I, 17:05 O) Time In = 07:50 • 1st BO = 12:00 • 1st BIN = 13:00 • Time Out = 17:05 Present (1 Lunch Break)
6 Punches (In, BO1, BIN1, BO2, BIN2, Out) All 6 time columns populated cleanly Present (2 Breaks Logged)
0 Punches (On Sunday with Mon-Sat schedule) All time cells blank Rest Day
0 Punches (On Tuesday with Mon-Sat schedule) All time cells blank Absent (Red Highlight)

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Quick solutions to common operational and hardware log issues.

Help Center
1. Why does an employee get "No active employee found with ID" on the portal? expand_more
Verify the following:
1. Ensure the employee is registered under the selected Company.
2. Verify that the Employee ID matches the record in the employee roster.
3. Confirm that the employee is marked active.
2. What happens if an employee forgets to clock out (Missing Time Out)? expand_more
If only one 'I' punch exists without an 'O' punch, the system records Time In and leaves Time Out blank. HR can review this via the Missing Logs Report in the Reports module.
3. How do I handle duplicate logs if the same file was uploaded twice? expand_more
The uploader contains built-in duplicate detection and skips previously imported lines automatically. If you wish to remove an entire upload batch, navigate to Manage Logs and click the red Delete button next to that session.
4. How do I change an account password? expand_more
Log in as an Administrator, navigate to User Accounts, locate the user, and click Edit to enter and save a new password.
5. Can the DTR sheet be printed directly without saving as PDF first? expand_more
Yes! Click the blue Print Sheet button at the bottom of the DTR preview. Your browser's print dialog will open immediately. Select your physical printer or choose "Save as PDF".
